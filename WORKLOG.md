## 2026-09-28 — whisper.cpp and ML Kit OCR verified on-device; a real whisper.cpp bug found and fixed

Root-caused by reading whisper.cpp's own source (`src/whisper.cpp`): `whisper_full_params.detect_language` is a **detection-only** mode — when true, `whisper_full` detects the language and `return 0`s immediately, **before transcribing anything**. Our bridge set `params.detect_language = (lang == "auto")`, so every "auto" request hit this early return. Fix: leave `detect_language` false always; `params.language = "auto"` alone already makes `whisper_full` detect-then-transcribe in one call (confirmed by the same source: the auto-detect branch only skips transcription when `detect_language` is explicitly set). After the fix: the same clip transcribes **word-for-word correctly** — "And so my fellow Americans ask not what your country can do for you, ask what you can do for your country." — in 17.2 s (11 s of audio, `ggml-base` q5_1, CPU).
