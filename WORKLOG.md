## 2026-09-28 — whisper.cpp and ML Kit OCR verified on-device; a real whisper.cpp bug found and fixed

Root-caused by reading whisper.cpp's own source (`src/whisper.cpp`): `whisper_full_params.detect_language` is a **detection-only** mode — when true, `whisper_full` detects the language and `return 0`s immediately, **before transcribing anything**. Fix: leave `detect_language` false always; `params.language = "auto"` alone already makes `whisper_full` detect-then-transcribe in one call (confirmed by the same source: the auto-detect branch only skips transcription when `detect_language` is explicitly set). After the fix: the same clip transcribes **word-for-word correctly** — "And so my fellow Americans ask not what your country can do for you, ask what you can do for your country." — in 17.2 s (11 s of audio, `ggml-base` q5_1, CPU). - **Caveat recorded honestly rather than papered over:** checked `/sys/devices/system/cpu/cpu*/cpufreq/scaling_cur_freq` after these runs and found every core well below its max frequency (e.g. the two fast cores at 1651 MHz of a 2304 MHz max) — DVFS/thermal scaling from several back-to-back heavy generation runs in one session, not a clean bench environment. That single confound is enough to explain the prefill regression above and means the *exact* decode numbers (4.00 vs ~5.2–5.4 tok/s) shouldn't be trusted to the tenth of a token/sec either. What *is* trustworthy: the direction was consistent across two independent nThreads=2 trials (5.42 and 5.20 tok/s, both clearly above the 4.00 tok/s baseline) despite the thermal noise, and the underlying reasoning (why llama.cpp exposes two separate thread-count parameters in the first place) holds regardless of this device's specific numbers. Shipped the split-parameter capability with `defaultDecodeThreadCount()=2` as a reasonable first default; flagged in STATUS.md that it needs a proper cooldown-controlled re-measurement, and a check on the Realme RMX2151, before being trusted as final tuning.

## 2026-09-28 — Real skill routing wired into Ask (ARCHITECTURE.md §5.1), and why laya doesn't apply
